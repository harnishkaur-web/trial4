Find the Planted Errors

You'll get a short document written by an AI tool. Somewhere inside it, a handful of details have been deliberately planted wrong. Your job: find them, and say what goes wrong if nobody catches them.

Brief

Mission rules

Work against the clock A visible timer runs while you search the document.
Flag, don't guess blindly Tap only the phrases you genuinely think are wrong.
State the consequence Finding an error isn't enough — say what it breaks.

Brief

Real life check

Why this small habit matters

This isn't just an exercise. The same mistakes show up in real work and real classrooms — and they're easy to miss if you're in a hurry.

At work A wrong figure in a note can mean the wrong stock gets ordered, or a client gets told the wrong thing.
In your studies A wrong date or name in an assignment can cost marks, even when the main idea was right.

Real life check

Your name on it

Once you send or submit something, it's yours — even if the AI wrote the wrong part. People remember careless work.

This is exactly why this lab is a mandatory, gated step in the course — it has to be practised properly before you move on, not just read about.
